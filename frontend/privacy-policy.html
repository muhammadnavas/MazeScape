Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy describes information associated with using MazeScape, an independent browser-based maze game.

Gameplay and information

MazeScape runs its gameplay in your browser. The game does not require registration or a player account. Maze layouts, player movement, ghost behaviour and in-game scoring are handled on the client. MazeScape does not upload game scores to a game server or operate an online leaderboard.

Browser storage

The game may use standard browser features and local storage for technical preferences or gameplay state where implemented. Information stored in your browser remains subject to your browser's site-data settings. You can clear site data through your browser, although this may reset saved preferences.

Advertising and Google services

MazeScape uses Google AdSense advertising services. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalise ads, subject to your consent choices, regional requirements and Google account or browser settings. Advertising providers may process information such as browser identifiers, device information and ad interactions.

Learn more in Google's information about advertising technologies and Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-party links

This website may link to external services such as GitHub. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Review their policies before sharing information with them.

Children's privacy

MazeScape is a general-audience browser game and does not require players to create an account or submit personal profile information to play. Please do not enter personal information into any optional browser or third-party service.

Your choices

You can manage cookies and clear site data using your browser settings. You can also use Google's available advertising controls. Disabling some storage or cookies may affect preferences or advertising functionality.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated if the game or its third-party services change. The current version will be published on this page with its latest update date.

Questions

For privacy-related questions, contact the project maintainer through the MazeScape contact page.
